V Notice
Smart campus notice board

Every notice.
Every screen.
In seconds.

V Notice lets any department publish to its own display from a phone, and lets students take the notice with them by scanning a QR code.

01 Post

Write, upload an image, video or voice message from any phone in about 30 seconds.

02 Show

Department screens update live. Urgent notices take over the display.

03 Scan

Students scan the QR code to read or save the notice on their phone.
